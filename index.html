HOOK

Language for Elevator Pitch

Learning Objectives

By the end of this section, you will be able to:

  • engage the audience by asking personalised questions
  • immerse the audience in a vivid scenario using scene starters
  • use appropriate language to hook your listener at the start of an elevator pitch

Apart from the four techniques introduced in Unit 2 (surprising facts/statistics, compelling questions, vivid stories, and citing quotations), you can engage the audience by asking personalised questions to connect to their experiences or challenges.

Examples:

"Are you tired of rough prototypes failing to capture the essence of your design?"
"Have you ever wondered why some exhibition displays captivate visitors in seconds?"
"How many of you have experienced screen-to-print colour mismatches ruining your final pieces?"
"Can you recall a time when hidden bugs crashed your game prototype during a playtest?"
"Are you tired of joining video calls that freeze at the most critical moments?"
"Have you ever wondered what prevents the entire Internet from collapsing?"
"How many of you have experienced irritating bugs in newly developed games?"
"Can you recall a time when a website failed you during an urgent transaction?"

Use scene starters to vividly immerse the audience in a scenario and spark their imagination.

Examples:

"Think about a performer with sensory issues putting on a sequinned costume that scratches all night."
"Imagine spending weeks on a costume collection, only to find that the fabric cannot withstand the performers' dynamic stage movements."
"Envision yourself unable to meet client expectations due to gaps between concept and execution."
"Picture yourself trying to protect your organisation's systems from ever-evolving cyber threats."
"Remember excitedly downloading a new app, only to have it crash the moment you opened it?"

Work in pairs. Choose a personalised question or scene starter above to hook your partner. Then switch roles.

You have 5 minutes to practise.

PROBLEM

Relating to the Audience

Learning Objectives

By the end of this section, you will be able to:

  • use useful verbs and nouns to describe problems
  • use present participle clauses to emphasise negative consequences
  • relate to the audience by acknowledging shared struggles
VerbNoun
struggle (with)challenge
grapple (with)concern
faceobstacle
encounterdifficulty
deal (with)struggle

Sentence Starters:

"Many of us have experienced ..."
"Time and again, we find ourselves ..."
"We've all faced moments when ..."
"It's a familiar situation when ..."

These clauses emphasise the negative consequences of a problem.

Problem: "...designers are stuck in endless email chains, painful revisions, and missed deadlines,"

Consequence: leading to / resulting in / bringing about / giving rise to wasted time, money, and creativity.

Problem: "...many companies still operate with outdated cybersecurity systems,"

Consequence: creating a greater risk of cyber attacks that could disrupt operations.

Problem: "...large language models (LLMs) may produce inappropriate or offensive responses,"

Consequence: generating safety concerns for users and platforms.

Create your own problem + consequence pair using a present participle clause. Share with your partner.

Pattern: [Problem], leading to / resulting in / creating [Consequence].

  • describe what makes your solution unique
  • emphasise the benefits to the audience
  • use key expressions to articulate your unique value proposition
"What makes ... unique is ..."
"What's special is ..."
"What really sets ... apart is ..."
"What distinguishes ... is ..."
"Unlike other options, ..."
"Our product is the only one that ..."
"Our unique value ... lies in ..."
"You'll benefit from ..."

CTA

Call to Action

Learning Objectives

By the end of this section, you will be able to:

  • use rhetorical questions to prompt reflection
  • use imperative sentences to urge the audience to act
  • end a pitch politely by expressing thanks
"What if ...?"
"Ready to ...?"
"Struggling to ...?"
"Tired of ...?"
"Get our new ... now and start making ...!"
"Adopt our latest ... today and enjoy ...!"
"Thank you."

Combine all three steps to create a complete call to action for your elevator pitch. Work in pairs.

Pattern: [Rhetorical Question] + [Imperative] + Thank you.

You have 5 minutes to practise.

CA SAMPLE

CA Sample Script — DataInsight Elevator Pitch

Overview

This is a sample elevator pitch script for the CA assessment. It demonstrates how to structure a pitch using the Hook, Problem, Solution, UVP, and Call to Action sections you have learned.

Self-introduction

"Hello, everyone. I'm Tony from Synix. At Synix, our mission is to empower businesses to transform data into actionable insights for informed decision-making."

Hook

"Have you, like many other business owners, struggled to extract meaningful insights from vast amounts of data?"

Problem statement

"The frustration of seeing valuable data sit idle knowing it could unlock business opportunities and drive success—it's an obstacle we know all too well."

Description of pain points using emotional appeal

"Many of us find it challenging to tap into the potential of underutilised data. Without effective analysis tools, we may overlook key patterns and trends, leading to missed opportunities, inefficient decisions, and lost competitive advantages."

Solution

"To address these challenges, I'm excited to introduce DataInsight, AI-powered software that makes data transformation effortless. Its real-time analytics enable instant data processing, while its predictive modelling helps you forecast trends and make informed decisions."

Unique value proposition (UVP)

"What makes DataInsight unique is its advanced AI-driven analysis. By rapidly processing complex datasets, it delivers accurate predictions, empowering your business with a competitive edge through data-driven decisions."

UVP — Why it is valuable to the audience

"With DataInsight, I'm confident that you will uncover hidden insights and achieve impactful outcomes."

Call to action

"Ready to turn your data into a powerful asset? Adopt DataInsight now and drive business growth."

Closing

"Thank you."

Overview 概述

Key notes, guidelines, and checklist for your CA Elevator Pitch assignment (25%). 以下為持續評估口頭簡報的關鍵指引及注意事項摘要。

1. 10 Essential Assignment Requirements 1. 十項基本作業要求

  • Weighting & Task: Individual speaking pitch (25% of total grade) for campus social media. 比重及任務：個人口頭簡報（佔總分25%），用於校園社交媒體。
  • Topic Focus: Apply discipline knowledge to improve or benefit daily lives. 主題焦點：應用學科知識來改善或造福日常生活。
  • Content Elements: State the problem/need, present the solution, and detail user benefits. 內容要素：陳述問題/需求，介紹解決方案，並詳述用戶益處。
  • Time Limit: Strict maximum of 1 min 30 sec; marks stop at 90 seconds. 時間限制：嚴格最長 1 分 30 秒；超過 90 秒後分數停止計算。
  • ID Statement: Read Name, Student ID, Class, and Campus before starting the timer. 身份聲明：在開始計時前宣讀姓名、學生編號、班級及校園。
  • One-Take Only: Single continuous recording; absolutely no video editing or audio dubbing. 一次過錄製：單一連續錄製；絕對不可進行影片編輯或音訊配音。
  • Equipment & Framing: Show face and upper body clearly; do not wear headsets or earphones. 設備及取景：清楚展示面部及上半身；不可佩戴耳機或耳塞。
  • Visual Aids: Mandatory visual aids; marked on effective usage, not visual design. 視覺輔助：必須使用視覺輔助；評分在於有效運用，而非視覺設計。
  • Delivery Style: Speak naturally and persuasively with eye contact; do not read from notes. 表達方式：自然而具說服力地說話並保持眼神交流；不可照稿宣讀。
  • Submission & Integrity: Submit via Moodle by Lesson 7, 23:55; strictly zero AI or plagiarism. 提交及誠信：經 Moodle 於第 7 課 23:55 前提交；嚴禁使用 AI 或抄襲。

2. Recommended Step-by-Step Recording Checklist 2. 建議錄製步驟清單

Step 步驟Action & Key Rules 行動及關鍵規則Notes / Verification 備註/確認
Step 1Environment & Framing Setup 環境及取景設定Quiet room, no background beeps; face and upper body clearly visible; remove all earphones/headsets. 安靜房間，無背景提示音；面部及上半身清楚可見；移除所有耳機/耳塞。
Step 2Record ID Message 錄製身份聲明Speak: 'My name is... My student number is... Class... Campus...'. This part is NOT counted in the 90s. 宣讀：「我的名字是...我的學生編號是...班級...校園...」。此部分不計入 90 秒內。
Step 3Start Timer & Begin Pitch 開始計時並開始簡報Press timer immediately after the ID message. Pitch length: max 1 min 30 sec. 身份聲明後立即按計時器。簡報長度：最長 1 分 30 秒。
Step 4Demonstrate Visual Aids 展示視覺輔助Hold up prototype, show slides, or point to visuals dynamically while speaking persuasively. 展示原型、投影片或在說話時動態地指向視覺輔助。
Step 5Conclude & Stop at 1:30 結束並在 1:30 前停止Deliver a concise wrap-up and stop speaking promptly before or at 90 seconds. Single take only. 簡潔地總結並在 90 秒前或剛好停止。僅限一次過錄製。

3. Evaluation Rubric & Marking Criteria 3. 評估標準及評分依據

Assessment Criterion 評估標準Focus & Expectations 重點及期望
1. Task Response 1. 任務回應Fulfilling all prompt specifications, addressing daily life benefits, and using visual aids effectively. 符合所有提示規格、回應日常生活益處，並有效使用視覺輔助。
2. Style 2. 風格Professional, engaging, and persuasive presentation style with confident posture and strong eye contact. 專業、引人入勝且具說服力的簡報風格，自信的姿勢及強勁的眼神交流。
3. Range of Vocabulary & Expressions 3. 詞彙及表達方式的廣度Appropriate workplace register, varied descriptive phrasing, and accurate terminology related to your discipline. 恰當的職場用語、多樣化的描述性措辭，以及與學科相關的準確術語。
4. Fluency & Cohesion 4. 流暢度及連貫性Natural, smooth delivery with logical transitions, avoiding script-reading pauses or unnatural hesitation. 自然而流暢的表達，具邏輯性的過渡，避免照稿宣讀的停頓或不自然的猶豫。
5. Pronunciation 5. 發音Clear articulation, appropriate stress, natural intonation, and audible vocal projection. 清晰的咬字、恰當的重音、自然的語調，以及可聽清的聲音投射。
6. Grammatical Accuracy 6. 文法準確度Consistently correct sentence structures, appropriate verb tenses, and high overall grammatical precision. 一致正確的句式結構、恰當的動詞時態，以及高度的整體文法精確度。

4. Quick Reference: DOs and DON'Ts 4. 快速參考：應做及不應做

✓ DO (Best Practices) ✓ 應做（最佳實踐）

  • Rehearse with a stopwatch to target 80–85 seconds. 用碼表練習，目標 80-85 秒。
  • Position your camera at eye level with bright front lighting. 將鏡頭置於眼睛水平位置，並有明亮的正面燈光。
  • Maintain consistent eye contact with the lens. 與鏡頭保持一致的眼神交流。
  • Speak expressively with clear voice modulation. 以清晰的聲音調節自然表達。
  • Seamlessly introduce and gesture towards visual aids. 無縫地介紹並指向視覺輔助。
  • Check video and audio playback before final submission. 提交前檢查影片及音訊回放。
  • Submit early on Moodle before Lesson 7, 23:55. 在第 7 課 23:55 前經 Moodle 提前提交。
  • Show your face and upper body. 展示你的面部及上半身。

✗ DON'T (Common Pitfalls) ✗ 不應做（常見錯誤）

  • DO NOT wear AirPods, headphones, or earphones. 不可佩戴 AirPods、耳機或耳塞。
  • DO NOT edit, trim, crop, or dub your video (one take only). 不可編輯、修剪、裁剪或配音你的影片（僅限一次過）。
  • DO NOT read from notes, scripts, or the task sheet. 不可照稿宣讀、讀稿或看任務紙。
  • DO NOT exceed 1 minute 30 seconds (overtime is ignored). 不可超過 1 分 30 秒（超時部分不予計分）。
  • DO NOT use AI tools (e.g., ChatGPT) to generate content. 不可使用 AI 工具（如 ChatGPT）生成內容。
  • DO NOT copy ideas or scripts from classmates. 不可抄襲同學的想法或稿子。
  • DO NOT record in noisy environments with beeping devices. 不可在有提示音的嘈雜環境中錄製。
  • DO NOT wear face masks. 不可佩戴口罩。
